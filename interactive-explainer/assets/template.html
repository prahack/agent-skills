How an order goes from the cart to the customer

The shop has three parts. The order part records what the customer buys. The payment part takes the money. The delivery part sends the parcel. Each part has its own service.

OrderA record of the items, the address and the total price.
ReservationStock that the shop keeps for one order until payment.
PaymentThe payment provider takes the money and sends a result to the shop.
ShipmentThe parcel and its tracking number from the courier.
